## Narrative Logic Check

Answer these before finalizing the sequence:

- **Opening figure**: Does it establish the problem / phenomenon clearly? (Not just "here's what exists" but "here's what's unknown")
- **Core figure**: Which figure would sink the paper if it were removed? It should be in the middle or after setup.
- **Closing figure**: Does it answer the question raised in the opening? (Avoid ending on a descriptive figure)
- **Transitions**: Between each pair of figures, can you write one sentence explaining why Fig N+1 follows from Fig N?
